# Moodboard: three directions for Plumbing By Paul

Inputs: `UNITED-ANATOMY.md` (the quality bar and the diagnosis), `21ST-COMPONENTS.md` (30 shortlisted components, numbered #1 to #30), `REFERENCES.md` (68 reference images in `refs/`), Paul's van photo and logo.

## Fixed for all three directions

**Colour comes from the vans, in the vans' proportions.** The vans are mostly white, with light blue second and navy third. Pixel samples (starting points, to be tuned in Figma): logo navy about `#101030`, van lettering navy about `#172839`, logo light blue about `#60a8c8`, lower-stripe blue about `#3b90b4`, crew shirt blue about `#75ade7`.
- Working ratio: about 60% white and near-white, 25% light-blue tints, 15% navy.
- No orange, red, coral, teal, cream or yellow. Stars in the Google rating may stay Google's own gold because that is Google's mark, not a brand colour.
- Light blue fails contrast for text on white. Text and primary buttons are navy; light blue is for fields, shapes, borders and highlights. A darker blue (near the stripe blue, darkened until it reaches 4.5:1 on white) is the link and accent-text colour.

**Type.** Paul's logo has two faces: blocky outlined capitals ("PLUMBING") and a brush script ("by Paul"). Neither was identified as a named font; this needs a font-match on the vector logo if Paul has one. Until then: the script is the brand's signature and becomes the "third voice" (used for 2 to 4 words at a time, never for sentences). The outlined block capitals stay inside the logo only.

**Assets on file.** Real: Paul portrait, Alycia portrait, two crew-and-trucks photos, two event photos, mascot logo. There are no real job photos. Three real Google quotes are on file out of 941. Any section that needs more (galleries, before/after, review wall) must show labelled placeholders until Paul or his Google listing supplies them.

**Carried over from United (craft, not looks):** 12 to 14 home sections, one overlap per section, eyebrow + two-line two-tone headline, three type voices, a CTA in every section, one motif repeated 6 or more times.

## Direction A: "Blue Shirts" (crew and fleet)

- **Mood words:** neighbourly, proud, uniformed, tidy, sunlit.
- **Idea:** the brand is 14 people in matching light-blue shirts in front of wrapped trucks. The site is designed like the livery and the uniform: white panels, light-blue bands, navy lettering, sewn name patches.
- **Colour use:** white page; hero and alternating sections on a very pale blue tint; the light blue at full strength only as shaped bands (the swoosh stripes from the van's lower panel) and as card fields; navy for all type, the footer card and one deep "24/7" section.
- **Type feel:** a sturdy, slightly condensed grotesque for headlines at medium-bold weight in sentence case (about 64 to 72 px for the hero, not 124), a neutral humanist sans for body, and the brush script for accents ("by Paul", "since day one", step numbers).
- **Motif:** (1) the **mascot roundel** used as a seal: on the hero photo corner, as the warranty stamp, as the map pin, as the loader. (2) **Name patches**: oval embroidered-style labels with a first name, on review cards ("fixed by Phil") and crew cards. (3) **Livery stripes**: the three flowing bands from the van as section edges.
- **Photo treatment:** real photos large, in rounded frames with a thin white border and soft shadow; light colour grade towards cool daylight; crew photo used wide. No cut-outs on blobs.
- **Five signature sections:**
  1. Hero: layered photo cards (crew wide, Paul smaller, roundel sticker) beside the headline; callback card overlapping the bottom edge; rating pill "4.9 · 941 Google reviews".
  2. "Who shows up": name-patch crew rail, each patch linked to a real review that names that technician.
  3. Trust bento: 4.9 score cell, water-meter counter rolling to 941, 1-year warranty seal, "answered on the first ring" quote cell, licence placeholder cell.
  4. Process as a pipe run: four job-ticket cards joined by a drawn pipe with fittings, step numbers in script.
  5. Pinellas map: SVG county with town shapes that light up on hover, roundel pin on St. Petersburg, town chips beside it.
- **Components:** #2 hero, #3 bento, #27 odometer, #9 lanyard rail (as patches), #5 review wall, #6 single quote, #4 process, #8 map, #1 tideline CTA, #10 footer, #16 and #17 header and menu, #30 town band.
- **References:** `refs/dribbble-092-wingly.png`, `refs/live-pimlicoplumbers-com.png`, `refs/live-mikediamondservices-com.png`, `refs/pinterest-42.jpg`, `refs/pinterest-56.jpg`, `refs/dribbble-103-rootover-home-services-w.png`.
- **Risks:** leans on crew photos, and only two good ones exist, so a short photo session would lift it a lot. The cartoon mascot can cheapen a refined layout if used large; keep it at seal size (64 to 120 px) except once. Name patches need Paul to confirm names and roles.

## Direction B: "Gulf Light" (coastal editorial)

- **Mood words:** calm, airy, clean water, morning light, considered.
- **Idea:** St. Petersburg is water on three sides. The page feels like clear water and daylight: lots of white, pale blue washes, a waterline that runs through the page.
- **Colour use:** white and the palest blue tints dominate (about 75%); light blue as soft gradients and one full-strength band; navy only for type, buttons and the footer.
- **Type feel:** a high-contrast serif for headlines in regular weight (as in `refs/dribbble-091-juan-pablo-devis.png` and `refs/dribbble-096-havenise-studio.png`), a light geometric sans for body, script for one or two words.
- **Motif:** the **waterline**: a gently moving wave edge that divides sections and rises through the final CTA; fine flowing lines in the hero background; ripple-light texture once.
- **Photo treatment:** fewer, larger photos with generous margins; cool, bright grading; fixtures and water close-ups alongside people.
- **Five signature sections:**
  1. Hero: serif headline over white with flowing-line background, one tall rounded crew photo, form card overlapping.
  2. Reviews as editorial pull quotes with a highlighter sweep on the key phrase.
  3. Services as an image accordion of four families.
  4. Story timeline with a line that fills like a pipe as you scroll.
  5. Tideline CTA: water rises to the review score.
- **Components:** #14 background paths, #11 or #12 hero, #20 highlight testimonial, #21 image accordion, #24 scroll timeline, #1 tideline CTA, #15 water caustic (once), #28 FAQ, #10 footer.
- **References:** `refs/dribbble-025-phenomenon-studio.png`, `refs/dribbble-031-phenomenon-studio.png`, `refs/pinterest-72.jpg`, `refs/dribbble-157-rejoanul-haque.png`, `refs/live-honeyhomes-com.png`.
- **Risks:** the furthest from the vans in feeling: a serif and soft washes can read as a spa or a pool company, and the mascot sits awkwardly in it. It depends on beautiful photography that does not exist yet (water and fixture close-ups would be stock). Lowest density of the three, so it could again be called "too simple".

## Direction C: "Shop Drawing" (technical and precise)

- **Mood words:** exact, engineered, trustworthy, methodical, crisp.
- **Idea:** the site looks like a plumber's plan set: fine navy line drawings of pipe runs, dimension ticks, job-ticket cards, stamped approvals.
- **Colour use:** white paper; light blue as blueprint-tint panels with a fine grid; navy line work and type; one solid navy section.
- **Type feel:** a tight grotesque for headlines, a monospace for labels, numbers and job data (the third voice here, with the script reduced to the logo and signature).
- **Motif:** **isometric pipe line drawings** that run between sections and connect cards; fittings as bullets; "inspected" stamps; numbered callouts on photos.
- **Photo treatment:** photos in square-cornered frames with callout labels and leader lines ("shut-off valve", "new PEX run"); needs real job photos to be honest.
- **Five signature sections:**
  1. Hero: headline left, a line-drawn cutaway of a Florida house's plumbing right, with hotspots that link to services.
  2. Services as a "symptom to fix" table of ticket cards.
  3. Process as a drawn pipe run with fittings as steps.
  4. Before/after sliders with callouts.
  5. County map as a drawn survey sheet with town labels.
- **Components:** #4 process, #7 before/after, #8 map, #3 bento, #6 single quote, #25 steps, #28 FAQ, #23 sticky cards, #13 hero.
- **References:** `refs/dribbble-031-phenomenon-studio.png` (line-drawn plan), `refs/pinterest-76.jpg`, `refs/dribbble-177-jacob-di-domenico.png`, `united/service-s03-cl.png` (symptom cards as a structure).
- **Risks:** needs custom illustration (the house cutaway is a real piece of work: Higgsfield or hand-drawn vectors) and real job photos for the callouts. Monospace + grid is the closest of the three to United's LED-and-data character, so it risks looking like a recolour of United. Cooler in tone than a family brand whose reviews praise friendliness.

## Recommendation: Direction A, "Blue Shirts", with the waterline borrowed from B

**Why A.**
1. **It is the only direction built from what Paul already owns.** The light-blue shirts, the white wrapped trucks, the roundel mascot and the script are on the street today. The site and the vans will look like one company, which is exactly the point Nick made about the colours.
2. **It fixes "too bold, too simple" at the root.** Light-dominant page, medium-weight sentence-case headlines at roughly half United's display size, and the detail count goes up through patches, seals, stripes, a rolling counter and layered photos. Calm from a distance, detailed up close.
3. **It uses Paul's strongest proof, which United does not have.** United has no Google reviews yet (its own site says "coming soon") and sells one owner's face. Paul has 4.9 from 941 reviews and a 14-person uniformed crew. A wall of real reviews tied to named technicians, a counter at 941 and the crew rail are things United's site cannot show.
4. **It needs the fewest assets that do not exist.** B needs water and fixture photography; C needs a commissioned cutaway drawing and job photos. A works with the photos on file today and gets better with a one-hour crew shoot.

**How it reaches United's level without looking like United.**

| | United | Paul, Direction A |
|---|---|---|
| Field | Dark navy dominant | White dominant |
| Display type | Condensed uppercase, 124 px | Sentence-case grotesque, about 64 to 72 px |
| Third voice | LED dot-matrix | Brush script from the logo |
| Motif | 24/7 LED sign | Mascot seal, name patches, livery stripes |
| Accent | Yellow | None warm; light blue does the work |
| Hero image | Owner in a night scene | Crew and trucks in daylight |
| Dividers | LED tickers, cyan wave lines | Livery stripes, moving waterline |
| Signature moment | LED phone number | Water-meter counter rolling to 941, tide rising in the final CTA |

Same discipline (section count, overlaps, three voices, a CTA per section, one motif used throughout), opposite look.

**Borrow from B:** the tideline as the final CTA and as two section edges, and flowing background lines in the hero at low contrast. **Borrow from C:** the pipe-run connector in the process section. Leave the serif and the monospace out.
