# Nick's voice persona (for every script)

Built 15 Nov 2026 from: Nick's own messages over the last 7+ days (about 29 messages in the content thread, the Plumbing by Paul thread, older project chat; ~38k words) and his 6 recorded audit takes (word-level transcripts, Nov batch). Use this for every script he has to say out loud. If a line wouldn't come out of his mouth naturally, rewrite it.

## Who he is on camera
"Hello, I'm Nick. Haha." Friendly, direct, a bit cheeky. A guy showing a buddy something on his screen, not a presenter. He talks like he types: fast, run-on, honest, with little reactions in the middle ("That's crazy." "I mean, come on.").

## How he builds a point (from his real takes)
1. Opens on the plumber + the odd thing, plain: "This plumber put his name on the first screen four times." / "Okay, so I've been on this plumber's website for a minute..."
2. Instantly reacts and points: "I mean, I'm not joking. Take a look." / "Let's take a look." / "Watch how far I have to scroll."
3. Walks through what he sees as a list: "We have the logo, then we have the head title, then we have the paragraph..." ("we have / then we have" is his signature).
4. One reaction line: "I mean, come on." / "That's crazy." / "Not one single face."
5. Says what it costs, through the customer: "somebody with a leak is definitely looking for a button, not a name" / "trust me, nobody's gonna look for a button while they have a leak".
6. Fix, as advice to a friend: "So why don't you..." / "So next time, do me a favor and..." / "Just swap X with Y and you'll see magic happen."
7. Ends on a short rhyming rule line: "Real faces win real jobs." "Trust first, then faucets second." "If they can't see it, they can't click it."

## Words he really uses (counted in his messages/takes)
- Fillers and reactions: I mean (most spoken), literally, basically, honestly, actually, definitely, of course, you know, come on, that's crazy, trust me, whatsoever, perhaps, anyways, yeah, right?, that's it.
- Phrases: "take a look", "let's take a look", "I'm not joking", "do me a favor", "so why don't you", "next time", "as of right now", "for example", "at the end of the day", "on top of that", "it's pretty simple", "you'll see magic happen".
- His own terms: "head title" (not headline), "call button", "homeowner", "the next guy", "their houses", "wanna", "gonna", "X compared to Y" (for ratios).
- Ratio of fillers: about one "I mean" / reaction per script, never more than two. They make it human; too many make it sloppy.

## Rhythm
- Medium sentences joined with "and", "so", "because". Not chopped into 3-word robot fragments.
- Questions to the viewer: "Who's getting the call?" "Right?"
- Talks to "you" (the plumber) directly, a little bossy in a friendly way.

## Never (sounds AI, he hates it)
- Same opener across scripts. Not "Plumbers, ..." on every one, not "I'm ..." on every one.
- Ultra-short staccato chains ("No links. Three names. Done."), neat triple parallel lists, "Here's the thing", "Let's dive in", "game-changer", "elevate", "seamless", "In today's world".
- Corporate words: utilize, leverage, optimize, solution, ensure.
- Generic "follow for more" or comment-bait endings (max 1 in 5 asks).

## Hook rule (Hormozi, still applies)
Every script: visual hook + on-screen text + spoken first line, all different per script, plumber named or obvious in the first 2 seconds, the strangest real detail first, proof on screen by 3 s.
