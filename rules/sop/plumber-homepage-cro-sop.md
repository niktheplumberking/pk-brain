**Owner:** Plumber King Agency (owned by Bennett & Co LLC)
**Version:** 1.3, 26 Oct 2026 (v1.3 adds: rule 7 in section 13, the 3 content blocks; assets column optional. v1.2 adds: lessons from Ed's 8 sites in section 12, the hard rules in section 13, the font register in section 11). v1.1 = Nick's answers to the 9 weak spots.
**Use it for:** every free-work and before-and-after design, and every paid client homepage.
**Scope right now [NICK]:** homepages only. The goal is to book at least 1 client. Town pages, service pages and speed get their own SOPs later.

## 1. The goal of the homepage

One job only: **get a homeowner to call or send the form.**

Everything on the page either:
1. builds trust (real owner, real reviews, real licences, real photos), or
2. removes effort (phone number and form always one tap away).

If a section does neither, it doesn't belong on the page.

## 2. Inputs to collect before designing

Collect all of these from the client's real website, Google profile and socials. **Never invent any of them.** [NICK][EZP]

| Item | Where to find it | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| Business legal name + trading name | site footer, Google profile | ask client |
| Owner first + last name, role, years in trade | About page | ask client |
| Owner photo (face, ideally in branded clothes) | About page, Facebook, Instagram, Google photos | follow the owner photo ladder in section 3B |
| Phone number | header | must have |
| Email | contact page | leave it off; don't make one up |
| Address / base town | footer, Google | must have |
| Opening hours + emergency hours | contact page | ask client |
| Service area towns | service-area page, footer | ask client |
| Services list (with real job photos for each) | services pages, gallery | use real job photos only |
| Licences, insurance, bonding, certifications | About / certifications page | only show what is written somewhere |
| Google rating + review count | Google Business Profile, checked live on the day | show "[count]" until checked |
| Facebook rating + review count | Facebook page | show "[count]" until checked |
| 3+ real review quotes, word for word, with name + town | reviews page, Google | quote word for word; mark any cut with "…" |
| Prices or fees they publish (service fee, diagnostic fee, coupons) | homepage, specials page | don't say "free quote" unless they really offer one |
| Real FAQs | FAQ page | write new ones from their real facts only |
| Brand colours, logo, fonts | site CSS, logo files | keep their logo; never redesign it |

**Rule:** before the design is shown to anyone, every number and claim must be traceable to a source. Keep a "facts used" list for each design. [EZP]

## 3. The 14-block homepage formula [ED]

Same order every time. The look can change (see section 6); the order and the job of each block do not.

### Block 01: Top bar
- Thin dark strip above the header.
- Shows: email or service area · "24/7" or emergency hours · trust tag (e.g. "Women-Owned") · social icons.
- Job: proves they're local and reachable before anything else loads.

### Block 02: Header
- Logo left, 5–6 menu links, **big phone button on the right** (number + "call or book online").
- Mobile: logo + phone icon button + menu button. The phone button must be the brightest thing in the header.

### Block 03: Hero (the most important block)
Must contain, top to bottom:
1. **Review chip**: platform logo + star rating + review count (e.g. "4.9 ★★★★★ 154 Google Reviews"). [ED]
2. **Eyebrow line** with the strongest credential ("Colorado Master Plumber · Since 1992"). [ED]
3. **H1 = [trade] in [town]**, with the trade word in the accent colour (e.g. "LOCAL **PLUMBER** IN BRIGHTON & FORT LUPTON"). This is the main local SEO line too. [ED]
4. **Area line**: "Serving X, Y & Z". [ED]
5. **2–3 line intro that names the owner and his years** ("…is run by Ken, a master plumber since 1992…"). [ED]
6. **Two buttons**: main action (Book / Get a quote) + phone number. [ED]
7. **3 trust icons**: licence, insured, one unique point (e.g. women-owned, guarantee). [ED]
8. **Owner photo on the right (desktop) or under the text (mobile)**: a cut-out of the owner in branded clothes, with a **name tag**: "NAME, Role". [ED]

**Pick the hero type first [NICK, 26 Oct]:**
- **Emergency-heavy plumber** (24/7, burst pipes, no-heat calls): the phone number is the main button, big "24/7 · Call now", response time in the eyebrow if they publish one.
- **Planned-work plumber** (water heaters, remodels, repiping): "Book online / Get a quote" is the main button, phone is second.
- Decide from their own site: what they lead with, their hours, their services list.

Never: a slogan as the H1 ("You dial, we go the extra mile"), stock photos, three equal buttons, or a job-application button in the hero. [EZP: these were Easy BreeZy's old mistakes]

### Block 03B: Owner photo ladder [NICK]

The hero always shows a full, finished photo of a person. Pick the first one that exists:

1. **The real owner** (best). No sticker needed.
2. **A real person from the same company**: family member, team member, technician. Use it. No sticker needed.
3. **Nobody real found**: generate one believable plumber with Higgsfield (ask Nick first if the run costs over 30 credits). Add the sticker.

**The sticker (only for a generated person, never for a real one) [NICK, 26 Oct]:** a small note with an arrow pointing at the photo that says **"Your photo goes here"**. Rules:
- It sits **inside the website frame** (on the design itself), because Nick screenshots the frame for content.
- Small and neat, so the design still looks finished. The photo stays fully visible.
- On both desktop and mobile.
- A generated person never carries the owner's real name in the name tag. Use the role only (e.g. "Your Master Plumber"). [inferred, my suggestion]

### Block 04: Quote / booking form card
- White card that overlaps the bottom of the hero (desktop) or sits right under it (mobile). [ED]
- Fields: Name, Phone, Email, ZIP/Postcode, "How can we help?", one button.
- Under the button: the real price rule if they have one (e.g. "$47 service fee · $57 diagnostic fee waived if you go ahead"). [EZP]
- Heading: "Book your plumber today" or "Get your free quote", **but only say "free" if it is free.**
- Keep two forms on the page: this one and the one in block 11. [NICK, 26 Oct]

### Block 05: Reviews
- Eyebrow + heading "Trusted by homeowners across [area]". [ED]
- **Platform chips first** (Google, Facebook, others) with stars + counts. [ED]
- Then 3 real review cards (desktop) or a 1-card slider (mobile): quote word for word, name, town, initial avatar. [ED]
- Where it fits the style, design it to look like a **live Google reviews widget** (Google logo, stars, "Verified" style row, "See all reviews" link) using the real reviews. In Figma it is static; on a live site it becomes a real widget. [NICK, 26 Oct]
- Sits on a brand-colour band so it stands out. [ED]
- Never add star ratings to a quote unless the source shows that rating. [EZP]

### Block 06: About
- Dark section. Heading with the years or the story ("A father & daughter plumbing company in Brighton"). [ED]
- Owner-at-work photo with a **"X+ years" badge**. [ED]
- 3 short paragraphs with accent bars: how it started · the owner's experience · how they treat customers. [ED]
- 2 buttons: "More about us" + main action.
- 4 value cards (icon + title + one line). [ED]

### Block 07: Services
- 8–11 cards. Each: icon + service name + one line + **real job photo** + "SEE [SERVICE]" button bar. [ED]
- Order: the money services first (water heaters, gas lines, repiping), small jobs last. [CRO, inferred]

### Block 08: Gallery / recent jobs
- "A look at the work". 2-row photo carousel on desktop, 2-column grid on mobile. [ED]
- Add a small caption per photo: job + town ("Frozen pipe · Dacono"). [EZP] This doubles as local proof.

### Block 09: Why choose us
- Dark section. Accordion of 5–6 trust points, first one open (licence, insured, guarantee/unique point, no upselling, clear pricing, emergency service). [ED]
- Owner or family photo next to it with a one-line caption. [ED]

### Block 10: Process
- 5 numbered steps: Call → Diagnosis/Quote → Clear price → Work done → Final check. [ED]
- One button under the steps.
- Use real price rules inside the steps if they exist. [EZP]

### Block 11: Contact
- Dark photo background. Heading names the town or the owner ("Need a plumber in Brighton?" / "Speak with David"). [ED]
- 4 contact cards: Call · Book/Email · Hours · Where we are. [ED]
- Full form again.

### Block 12: FAQ
- 5–6 questions, first one open. [ED]
- Always include: fees/quotes · licensed & insured · emergency response time · 2–3 service questions from their own FAQ.

### Block 13: Areas we cover
- Map + list of 10–20 towns + "Call now" button. [ED]
- **The map is ALWAYS a real Google Maps screenshot of their service area, placed as a picture. Never generated, never drawn, never OpenStreetMap.** [NICK] (Ed's live sites embed a real Google Maps iframe: fact, seen in the code of all 8.)
- Every town name spelled correctly (check it). [EZP]

### Block 14: Footer
- Logo + short company blurb + trust badges (BBB, certifications) · quick links · services list · contact details with icons · socials. [ED]
- Copyright bar + "Website by Plumber King Agency". [ED does the same with his agency]

## 4. Rules that apply to every block

1. **Phone number on every screen**: top bar, header, hero, contact, areas, footer, and a sticky call bar on mobile. [ED][NICK][EZP]
2. **The owner's face and first name everywhere**: hero, about, why-us, contact heading. Homeowners hire a person, not a logo. [ED]
3. **Trade + town** in the H1, in section headings, and in the page title tag. [ED]
4. **Every section follows the same pattern**: small eyebrow with brand icons → big uppercase heading in 2 colours → 2-line intro → content. [ED]
5. **Light and dark sections alternate** so the page has rhythm. [ED]
6. **One accent colour** does the highlighting (headline words, buttons, icons). [ED]
7. **A brand divider motif** between some sections (flames for heating, hazard stripes for Heat Lord, a water wave for Easy BreeZy). [ED][EZP]
8. **Real photos only**: owner, team, vans, jobs. No stock. The only AI person allowed is the labelled stand-in from the owner photo ladder (block 03B). [ED][NICK]
9. **Main button contrast**: white text on the accent colour must pass 4.5:1. If the brand colour is too light, use a darker shade of it. [EZP]
10. **No invented facts**: no fake ratings, badges, guarantees, prices or years. Anything unconfirmed shows as "[count]" or goes in the "check before live" list. [NICK]
11. **US clients: US spelling** (license, neighbors, color). UK clients: UK spelling. [EZP]

## 5. Mobile rules [ED][EZP][NICK]

**Nick's rule (26 Oct):** the mobile design is simply the mobile version of the desktop design we made. Page length does not matter. What matters is that it is conversion-optimized and shows everything the business really does.

Checked on Ed's two sites at phone size (390 px) on 26 Oct 2026.

- Width 390 px, 20 px side padding.
- Header: logo + phone button + menu button.
- Hero order: review chip → eyebrow → H1 (3 short lines) → area line → intro → full-width buttons stacked → trust icons as a list → owner photo with name tag **under** the text.
- Form fields stack one per row.
- Reviews: one card at a time with arrows.
- Services: one card per row, full width.
- Gallery: 2-column grid, 6 photos.
- Value cards: 2 per row.
- **Sticky bottom bar**: "Call now" + "Book online", always visible. [EZP: Ed uses a WhatsApp bubble instead]
- Check every heading for bad line breaks (one word alone on a line).

## 6. Make every design look different [NICK]

The structure stays. The look must change so our work isn't recognisable as a template. Change at least **4** of these per project:

| Element | Options to rotate |
|---|---|
| Colour mood | client brand colours · dark hero vs light hero · gradient vs flat |
| Heading font | condensed display (Bebas) · geometric bold · serif |
| Hero layout | owner right / owner left / owner centred with text over · split screen · full-bleed photo |
| Form | overlapping card · side panel in the hero · full-width strip |
| Review layout | 3 cards · 1 big quote + small ones · scrolling strip · Google-style list · photo + quote |
| Services | photo cards · icon grid · alternating rows · tabs |
| Why us | accordion · icon grid · numbered list · big stats row |
| Process | horizontal steps · vertical timeline · zig-zag |
| Divider motif | from the client's trade or brand (water, pipes, flames, tools) |
| Corners/shapes | sharp · rounded · angled cuts |

Keep a log per design of what was used, so the next one picks differently.

## 7. Process, step by step

1. **Get the link** to the client's bad site from Nick.
2. **Audit it**: screenshot desktop + mobile, list what breaks the formula (slogan H1, hidden reviews, no owner, weak phone CTA…). Use REA only if it helps. [NICK]
3. **Collect the inputs** in section 2, with sources.
4. **Pick the style** (section 6), different from the last designs.
5. **Figma**: new page in the Plumber King designs file, **named after the company**. Layout: **assets + components on the left, desktop homepage in the middle, mobile homepage on the right. Nothing else.** [NICK] **Update 26 Oct 2026 [NICK]:** the assets/components column is optional ("nobody cares"). Every page MUST get the 3 content blocks from rule 7 in section 13.
6. **Build desktop** (1440 px) block by block, then **mobile** (390 px).
7. **QA** (section 8).
8. **Report to Nick**: link, what changed vs the old site, and the "check before live" list.

- [ ] H1 = trade + town; owner named in the hero intro.
- [ ] Phone visible on every screen; sticky call bar on mobile.

- [ ] Process line passes THROUGH the step numbers/circles; no text overlaps anything.
- [ ] Every quote word for word; cuts marked with "…".
- [ ] Every photo is the client's own; none used twice in the same block.
- [ ] Hero person follows the photo ladder; if it is a generated person, the "Your photo goes here" sticker is inside the frame on desktop and mobile.

- [ ] No "free" unless it's free; prices match their site.
- [ ] Spelling: town names, US vs UK.

## 10. Posting concept redesigns publicly (inferred, not legal advice)

Nick has a lot of freedom to design and post concepts, but not total freedom, because the designs use another business's name, logo, photos and reviews. To keep it safe and honest:

- Never say or imply they are a client unless they signed. Captions say "free redesign" or "concept", not "my client". [CRO, inferred]
- A stand-in person always carries the "Your photo goes here" sticker, so nobody thinks a fake person is the real owner. [NICK]
- Never change their reviews or ratings, and never add claims they don't make.
- If a business asks for a post to come down, take it down.

## 12. Lessons from Ed's 8 sites [ED8, studied 26 Oct 2026]

Sites: advancedheating.info (AH), heatlord.com (HL), southerndrainageandwater.co.uk (SD), comfyheatingandplumbing.co.uk (CH), heyplumber.co.uk (HP), onecallplumbingsolutions.co.uk (OC), unpluggedplumber.co.nz (UP), rightflow.co.uk (RF). Studied by 3 separate reviewers with screenshots at desktop and mobile. Site tags = where a reviewer saw it (fact); a missing tag means not checked, not absent. The conversion value of each is **inferred** (we have no numbers). HP and RF were studied but their detail is folded into the shared patterns.

**Structure: same skeleton, different skin.** All 8 follow the 14-block formula. What makes each look premium is the craft below, not a new layout. Fact.

Hero
1. Review chips from 2–3 real platforms (Google, Facebook, Checkatrade/Yelp) with counts, plus a combined total ("4.9 ★ 180+ reviews"). (AH, SD, CH, OC)
2. The owner's real world behind him: their van, crew, a local landmark, plus a name tag ("BEN / OWNER & ENGINEER"). (AH, HL, SD, OC, CH)
3. One or two words of the headline in the accent colour or a two-colour/gradient fill. (AH gradient, UP gradient, OC, SD, CH two/three-colour)
4. An offer or guarantee ribbon near the form ("No call-out fee", "Fixed price"), and one reassurance line under the form button ("No hidden charges"). Only if true for the client. (CH finance strip + reassurance, SD guarantees, UP free health check)

Craft that reads as "premium"
5. Each face has one job (headings, eyebrows, buttons, numbers). Note: Ed himself REUSES fonts (Montserrat on both OC and CH). We don't: section 11. (AH, HL, OC, SD)
6. One bespoke divider motif (wave, pipe, drip, angle) reused 3+ times down the page. (AH flame border, OC red swoosh, SD wave)
7. Angled or clipped panels and photo corners. (AH header, CH top bar, OC photo frames, UP band edge)
8. Framed eyebrows: small label above each heading, in a pill, box, with rules or icons each side. (AH, HL, UP, SD mono)
9. Badges stuck onto photos ("Gas Safe", "15 yrs") — but never over a face (section 13). (AH, CH, UP)
10. Owner signature under the About text, and 3–4 stat tiles (years, jobs, reviews). (signature SD; stat tiles CH)
11. Process: numbered circles joined by a connector line that runs THROUGH the circles, not beside them. (AH, UP, SD, OC)
12. Review cards with craft: Google logo, stars, name, town, date, a short real quote. (AH, OC, SD, CH)
13. Background texture so big sections aren't flat: a huge faded watermark word, a subtle pattern, or a soft colour glow. (AH dot grid, OC diamond grid, UP line pattern, SD line-art)
14. One shape language everywhere: if corners are round, everything is round; if sharp, everything is sharp. (HL all square; AH all 10 px)
15. Motion is restrained: fade-up on scroll, a slow logo/credential marquee. No spinning or bouncing. (AH: no CSS animation at all, only carousels)

Trust and closing
16. Emergency banner right before the footer: big phone number, "24/7" only if true. (inferred, not confirmed on these sites; phone CTA after every section is confirmed on SD)
17. Badge wall of real accreditations, only when the client really has them. (SD brand-logo band; credentials in copy on OC)
18. Marquee strip of credentials. (UP)
19. Gallery tiles labelled with the town of the job ("Taradale, Hawke's Bay"). (UP)

Mobile (seen on OC, SD, CH, UP)
20. Drop the top bar; header = logo + call pill + menu, sticky on OC and CH (SD is not sticky).
21. Order in the hero: headline → copy → review chips → call button → owner photo → form.
22. Note: Ed has no sticky bottom call bar. Ours is our own addition; keep it, but test it never covers content.

## 13. Hard rules added 26 Oct 2026 [NICK]

1. **Areas we cover = a real Google Maps screenshot.** Open Google Maps on their service area, screenshot, place it as an image. Never generate it, never draw it, never use OpenStreetMap. Name the layer "map (Google Maps screenshot, <date>)". If the cloud browser can't load Google Maps, take it on Nick's PC through Remote Control.
2. **Ratings = the real Google numbers.** Read the stars and review count from the client's Google profile and write them everywhere a rating shows (hero chip, review widget, footer). Date them in the layer name. If Google blocks the cloud browser, read them in Nick's PC browser through Remote Control (worked 26 Oct 2026 for Smart Plumbing: 4.9, 115 reviews). Never leave "[count]" in a finished design.
3. **Nothing covers a face.** Badges, stickers and chips go on a layer BEHIND the person cut-out, or beside them. Check the screenshot.
4. **Fonts:** the client's own fonts if he has them; otherwise premium fonts no earlier client used, that look different too (section 11, rule of 23 Nov 2026).
5. **No text overlaps** other text, photos or lines. Process lines pass through the numbers.
6. **Never say something can't be done without testing it live first**, using every route including Remote Control on Nick's PC.
7. **Every design page gets 3 content blocks** (Nick, 26 Oct 2026, for his content videos; style from the reel instagram.com/p/DeyfWguCIs2): **01 Old vs New Sitemap** (pill lists; old = their real current homepage sections, new = ours with NEW tags and a "X blocks → Y blocks" note), **02 Brand Moodboard** (logo tile, real photos only, "Aa" font tile, their real tagline + owner signature tile, real Google rating tile, icon tile), **03 Colors & Fonts** (big heading-font "Aa" left, body font below, 4 brand colours with HEX + CMYK right). Each 1320 wide; copy the layout from Plumbing by Paul (frames 112:1528 / 112:1621 / 112:1721) and restyle to the client's fonts and colours. Position on the page doesn't matter.
