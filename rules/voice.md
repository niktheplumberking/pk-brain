## Website audit reels are NOT /ig-audit

"Audit" here means a reel that critiques a plumber's website. `/ig-audit` is
only for reviewing Nick's own post stats.

Website audit reels follow, in order, the files
`rules/audit/AUDIT-SCRIPT-WORKFLOW.md` and
`rules/audit/AUDIT-STEP-PROMPTS.md`:

1. Research the website, Google Business Profile and reviews.
2. Match against Ed's 36-flaw list (`rules/ed-flaw-list.md`). Verified flaws only.
3. Jev picks 1 to 3 flaws. Maximum 5 Jev calls per task.
4. Short private report with proof.
5. Own-voice script.
6. Upgrade to Ed's voice. Deliver only this step.

Always judge from desktop view (1440 px). Every sentence must match what is on
screen at that moment. Check every fact: "4.9 stars from 1,624 reviews", never
"1,624 five-star reviews". Open on the plumber and the odd detail, worded
differently per script, then the flaw. No ask at the end. Frame it as how to fix it, not just "this site is bad".

hookscore rates these audit openers low because they are long. Ignore its
verdict on audit hooks. Accuracy to the screen comes first.
